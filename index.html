NEON MAZE

CYBER ARCADE EXPERIENCE

HOW TO PLAY

OBJECTIVE

Collect all fragments to open the portal to the next level. Avoid the VOIDERS at all costs.

CONTROLS

  • WASD or ARROWS to move.
  • Mobile: Use the invisible virtual joystick on the screen.
  • ESC to pause or go back.

POWER-UPS

  • ⚡ OVERDRIVE: Doubles speed.
  • 👻 PHASE: Move through walls.
  • 💥 PULSE: Stuns enemies.
  • 🌀 PORTAL KEY: Activates portal early.

ENEMIES

  • CHASER: Hunts you directly.
  • SHADOW: Ambushes you.
  • RUSHER: Fast and erratic.
  • GUARDIAN: Protects zones.
